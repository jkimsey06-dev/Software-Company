# Outreach email

Draft only. The owner reviews, edits and sends. Never send, schedule or queue an email.

## Voice
Short, plain, local. Sounds like a person from the area, not a marketing tool.
Until a voice profile exists, ask the owner for two or three emails they've written
and match them.

## Each draft
- Subject: under 6 words, specific to the business
- 60 to 120 words
- One specific, verifiable observation about their business (from the audit or public info)
- One sentence on what the missed-call machine would do about it
- One low-pressure ask (a 15-minute call, or "want me to send the one-page audit?")
- Signature with the sender's name and business
- **Physical mailing address** (required)
- **Working unsubscribe line**, e.g. "Not interested? Reply 'no thanks' and I won't email again."

## Never
- Claim results, rankings or numbers you can't back up
- Mention pricing below the floors, or invent discounts
- Use SMS or AI voice for outreach. Email, live human calls and in person only
- Draft to anyone who has opted out. Check the opt-out list first if it exists

Flag anything that looks like a CAN-SPAM or other compliance risk for the owner to
check with a lawyer.
